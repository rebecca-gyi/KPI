Speed

From your emails to Keith. Minutes per quote is the gap between one quote email and the next on the same day (gaps over 2 hours left out, as breaks). Turnaround counts working days from when a quote could start: the later of Keith's request and 60 days before road tax expiry (insurers won't quote earlier). Weekends don't count. Covers threads Keith started, plus threads you started where his request email for that plate or company could be found. Requests sent in bulk emails, attachments or WhatsApp can't be timed, so those quotes only count towards minutes per quote.
